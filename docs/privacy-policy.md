# Privacy Policy — Nova Stack

## Data we collect ourselves
None. The App has no accounts and no servers of our own. Your progress (scores,
streak, power-ups, settings, puzzle stars, achievements, purchase status) is
stored **only on your device** and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

## Third-party services
**Google AdMob** (advertising). AdMob may collect device identifiers (Android
Advertising ID, or the iOS IDFA if you allow tracking), IP address and
approximate location, app interaction and diagnostic data, in order to show,
limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Details:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**RevenueCat** (in-app purchases). Processes your purchase receipts and a random,
anonymous app user ID so purchases can be validated and restored. No name or
email is collected. Details: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

**Apple App Store / Google Play** process payments under their own policies.
We never see your payment details.

## Consent and tracking
- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** on first launch the App shows Google's consent
  form (IAB TCF). You can change your choice any time in
  **Settings → Privacy & ad choices**.
- **iOS:** the App asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency).
  If you decline, ads are not personalized using the IDFA. You can change this
  in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

## Removing ads
The optional "Remove Ads" purchase turns off banner and interstitial ads.
Rewarded videos stay available, and are only shown when you choose to watch one.

## Children
Nova Stack is a general-audience game, not directed at children under 13. Ad
requests use a G/PG maximum content rating. If you believe a child's data was
collected, contact us and we will help.

## Your rights
Because we store no personal data on servers we control, there is nothing for
us to export or delete. To request deletion of RevenueCat purchase records, or
for any privacy question, contact us below. Uninstalling the App deletes all
local data.
