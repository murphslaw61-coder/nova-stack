## Full description

Drop planets, merge matching ones, and grow the cosmos from a humble Asteroid all
the way to a blazing Supernova. But every few drops, gravity flips and the whole
board comes crashing the other way.

MERGE THE COSMOS
Touch two matching planets to fuse them: Asteroid → Moon → Mercury → Mars → Earth
→ Neptune → Saturn → Supernova. Group four or more for a MEGA MERGE that jumps two
tiers. Chain reactions cascade into huge combos.

GRAVITY FLIPS. PLAN FOR IT.
Watch the flip meter. When gravity reverses, everything slides to the other side,
and every merge from that slide scores a bonus. Turn chaos into your best combo.

BIG BANG
Build a Supernova, then crash two together. The explosion obliterates everything
around it for a massive score.

METEOR SHOWERS
The longer you survive, the harder space fights back. Dark Matter rains onto the
board. It never merges, so shatter it by merging right next to it.

SIX WAYS TO PLAY
• Endless: how long can you survive?
• Daily: one board for everyone, every day. Build your streak and earn free power-ups.
• Weekly: a new rule twist each week
• Puzzles: 30 hand-tuned levels with 3-star ratings
• Zen: no flips, no game over, just merging
• Extreme: smaller board, faster flips, no power-ups

POWER-UPS
Bomb, Shuffle, Gravity Lock and Delay get you out of tight spots. Earn them from
achievements and daily play, or by watching an optional video.

GORGEOUS AND SATISFYING
Hand-crafted planets, particle bursts, shockwaves and a calm space soundtrack.
Unlock the Golden Hour and Neon Void planet skins.

Plays offline. No account needed.
